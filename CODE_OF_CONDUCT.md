# Code of conduct

callscope follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated anywhere in the project's spaces.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainer through the contact options on [Vigneshselvaraj1811's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Vigneshselvaraj1811). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.
